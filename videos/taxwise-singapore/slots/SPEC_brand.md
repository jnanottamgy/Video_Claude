Read `slots/BRIEF.md` first. The TW mark (`design/tw-mark.js`) is the star of this reel; you own its biggest
moments. Make the logo animation signature-worthy: the client loved a previous reel where the brand's logo
animation was used everywhere.

## 1. `singapore_slam` (full) window 12.500-14.220 = 52 frames

the film.
- beats: 12.50* 13.00 13.50 14.00
- words: into@12.37 Singapore.@12.53 (the word lands on the first frame and rings to ~13.2)
- The compositor adds a white-gold flash, shake and chromatic kick on 12.50 over your first frames. Start
  bold: frame 1 must already be a strong, full-frame image (not an empty navy).

Direction:
- **SINGAPORE**, gigantic, slams in letter by letter, all landed by ~12.85 with overshoot and motion blur:
  - gold metal, Montserrat 900 or Cormorant 700 (your call);
  - it fills the width at about y 820-1040.
- **NOW IN** (tracked caps, ivory) snaps in above it on 13.00.
- The TW mark (~200 px) above, assembling fast on 12.50-12.90.
- A gold location pin with expanding pulse rings marks the "i" or sits below.
- Behind it all:
  - a navy ground with light rays bursting from the centre (gold, subtle royal-blue rim);
  - fine gold particles thrown outward on 12.50.
- **13.50:** a light sweep across the type.
- **14.00-14.22:** prepare the hand-off. Everything accelerates into the camera (scale up and blur, or the TW
  shield zooming through the lens) so the next full scene (the team) can start on 14.22 from navy.
